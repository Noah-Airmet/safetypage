**Report: SafetyPage design B, UI craft review, 2026-11-10 03:55 capture**

Measured with the browser unless marked "judgment". The site's own stylesheet states its rule at the top: every value is one of the tokens in `:root` (type 12/14/16/17/20/28/44, spacing 4/8/12/16/24/32/48/64/96). I cite that where a finding breaks it.

**Findings, most to least severe**

1. **connect, 390. `.routes` table.** The third column (response type) is cut to one letter at the right edge: "S", "D", "U", "Q", "A". The table is 455px wide in a 358px column and sits in an overflow-auto wrapper, so it scrolls, but nothing signals that and the clipped glyphs read as broken. Measurement: `td:last-child` x=366.9, w=103.8, right=470.7; viewport 390. Screenshot: `connect-390-closed.png`. Fix: let the path cell wrap on phones (drop `white-space: nowrap` under 700px) or stack the response under the path as a second line.

2. **home, 390. `.topics > a > .sub`.** In the last row the two subtitles sit at different heights because the grid cells stretch to the row's height and "Evaluation awareness" wraps to two lines: "48 cards, 4 labs" top=2514.2, "23 cards, 2 labs" top=2526.7, a 12.5px offset between neighbours. Screenshot: `home-390-closed.png` (section "One topic"). Fix: `align-content: start` on `.topics a`, or align the sub to the cell bottom so a wrapped name pushes its own sub down only.

3. **shelf-filtered, 390. `.filters select.field`.** A row of three selects wraps two-and-one: Publisher 175px and Year 175px on one line, then "Read anywhere" alone at 358px full width. Measurement: selects at x=16 w=175, x=199 w=175, then x=16 w=358. The labs shelf has four selects (2+2) and the search page four (2+2), so only this page looks broken. Screenshot: `shelf-filtered-390-closed.png`. Fix: give the selects a fixed 45% basis without growing (`flex: 0 1 45%`) so an odd one stays half-width, or make all three stack full-width.

4. **connect, 1440. `.anatomy` (two side by side).** The two example citations touch: the first ends at x=538.3 and the second starts at x=538.3. The only space between "p6" and "distill:zoom-in" is the 8.6px margin on the last code, which equals the gap between the parts inside one citation (388.9→397.5, 414.8→423.5). Two separate things read as one string "p6 distill:zoom-in". Screenshot: `connect-1440-closed.png`. Fix: put a 48px gap between anatomies, or stack them as the phone layout already does.

5. **home, 1440. `#agents-h` and the right column `.rows`.** Three heading-to-content rhythms on one page: Library h2 line box bottom 834 → first row text 858 (24px), "One topic" h2 bottom 1492.6 → first name 1520.6 (28px), "For agents" h2 bottom 1748.8 → codeline 1764.8 (16px). And the right column of "For agents" has no heading, so a 20px row name "Check a paragraph" starts 12px below a 28px h2 with baselines ~5px apart (1747.4 vs 1742.5). The cause is `.section > .h2` only matching a direct child, so the two split sections get different margins. Screenshot: `home-1440-closed.png`. Fix: one rule for h2-to-content (24px), applied whether or not the h2 is wrapped, and either a heading over the right column or move its first row down to the codeline's top.

6. **check, 1440 and 390. `textarea#check-text`.** The example text is cut mid-line at the bottom edge: rows=6 gives clientHeight 195 but the content is 250 tall, so the seventh line shows half-clipped inside the bottom padding. Screenshots: `check-1440-closed--6-form.png`, `check-390-closed.png`. Fix: size the textarea to the example (rows that fit, or auto-grow via `field-sizing: content` with a max).

7. **search, 1440. `.search .meta .cite`.** The mono cite sits on the meta line in 19 results and drops to its own right-aligned line in 6 of 25, so the rows have two different shapes. When inline it also sits ~3px above the meta baseline because `.meta` aligns stretch, not baseline (cite box bottom 595.1 vs "Paper" 599.1). Screenshot: `search-1440-closed.png`. Fix: `align-items: baseline` on `.meta`, and either always give the cite its own line or let it shrink with `min-width: 0; overflow: hidden; text-overflow: ellipsis`.

8. **shelf, search, follow, home, 1440. Document titles in lists.** The same thing, a document's title in a list row, is set at three sizes: 20px/500 on the home rows (`.rows .name`), 17px/500 in search results (`.search .doc`) and follow cards (`.card-body > .name`), 16px/500 in the catalogue (`.cat .name`). Measured fonts: 20/25, 17/22.95, 16/22.4. Screenshots: `home-1440-closed--5-wrap.png`, `search-1440-closed--3-wrap.png`, `shelf-1440-closed.png`. Fix: pick two (a list name and a dense-table name) and use them everywhere.

9. **reader-text, 1440. `.textdoc .title`.** The document title is 32px, a size off the token scale (tokens are 28 and 44), and the same document's title is 44px on its record page and 28px on a source card. Measurement: `500 32px/34.56px`. Breaks the stylesheet's "every value is one of the tokens" rule. Screenshot: `reader-text-1440-closed.png`. Fix: use `--t-28` to match the source card, or `--t-title` at the phone size (32 = `--t-title` under 700px, so a token already exists if that's the intent).

10. **reader-source-card (and the other source cards), 1440 and 390. `.qc-result .state` vs `.qc-result .muted`.** After a quote check, "word for word" sits 2px below "On p. 135": inline boxes 555.3–573.3 vs 557.4–575.4 at 1440, 569.1–587.1 vs 571.3–589.3 at 390. The state is inline-flex (centred with its icon) and the following text is a plain inline, so they don't share a baseline. Screenshot: `reader-source-card-1440-closed--5-quotecheck.png`. Fix: make `.qc-result` a flex row with `align-items: baseline`, as `.qstatus` on the PDF reader already does (there the state and quote boxes both start at 64).

11. **replay, 1440. `.rp-tally`.** The top-right tally (20px) aligns with neither line beside it: its baseline is ~96, the lead "A document in the library…" is ~88 and the title is ~118. Measurement: lead inline box 73–91, tally 75–101, h1 96.3–122.3. Screenshot: `replay-1440-closed--5-rp-head.png`. Fix: align the tally's baseline to the title's first line (drop the 2px padding-top and use `align-items: baseline` on `.rp-head`, with the tally on the h1 row), or to the lead.

12. **replay, 390. `.book-name`.** "Cunningham et al. 2023" is clipped to "Cunningham et al. 2" with no ellipsis: scrollWidth 147 vs clientWidth 120, and `text-overflow: ellipsis` does nothing because the element is `display: flex`. Screenshot: `replay-390-closed.png`. Fix: wrap the text in a span that carries the ellipsis, or make `.book-name` block with `line-height: 24px`.

13. **check, both widths. `.head .tally` (legend) and `.summary.tally`.** The legend "Verified · Page only · Rejected" under the title repeats the summary "1 verified · 1 page only · 3 rejected" 330px lower, in the same component, and explains what the marks mean rather than showing it. 14. **citationbench, 1440. `.board th .src`.** The small sub-label row under the headers means "which source scored this" (code, judge), but the "$ per correct" column uses it for the word "answer", the end of the header, so one column's label is split across two styles. Judgment. Screenshot: `citationbench-1440-closed.png`. Fix: "$ per correct answer" on one line (or "Cost / correct") with the sub-label blank.

15. **search and shelf, 1440. `select.field`.** A row of four selects at four content-driven widths: 304, 182, 251, 151px (search); 300, 182, 112, 143px (labs shelf). Measured.
