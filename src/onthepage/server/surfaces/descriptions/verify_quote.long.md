Check a quotation before you use it. Pass the exact words you intend to put in quotation marks, and the cite (id or url) you intend to attach. Join omitted stretches with "..." as you would in print.

Verdicts: exact and normalized pass (normalized forgives case, punctuation, hyphenation and line breaks, never wording). elsewhere means the words are in that document but not at the cited place; the result gives the right place. near_miss means something close is there but not these words; the result shows the source's own words and the difference, so quote those instead. not_found means the words are not there.

Without cite, pass doc to check against a whole document, or neither to search the whole library for the passage. The check is code, not a model: deterministic and exact.
